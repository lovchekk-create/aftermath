Grand Theft Auto VI is set in the fictional state of Leonida — Rockstar's take on Florida — and returns to Vice City, the series' sun-soaked answer to Miami. It follows a criminal duo, Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, whose lives collide after an easy score goes wrong.
